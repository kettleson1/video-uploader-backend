Ruleset: NCAA college football.
Source basis: 2025 NCAA Football Rules and Interpretations, Rule 9, Section 3, Article 3.

Offensive holding occurs when a Team A player uses hands, arms, or body control to materially restrict an opponent, especially by grasping, hooking, tackling, pulling, turning, or preventing separation. The foul should have a visible effect on the defender's ability to pursue, disengage, or make a play.

Not every hand placement is holding. Legal blocking allows open-hand contact within the frame when the blocker does not grab, restrict, or take the opponent to the ground. Consider point of attack, defender's path to the ball, and whether the restriction creates an advantage.

Video keys: grab or hook, jersey stretch, arm outside the frame, takedown, defender's leverage and path, point of attack, and whether the contact continues after the defender attempts to disengage.
