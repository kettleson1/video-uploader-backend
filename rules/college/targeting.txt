Ruleset: NCAA college football.
Source basis: 2025 NCAA Football Rules and Interpretations, Rule 9, Section 1, Articles 3 and 4; 2026 targeting disqualification administration changes should be applied separately from the foul decision.

Targeting includes forcible contact with the crown of the helmet, or forcible contact to the head or neck area of a defenseless opponent, when at least one targeting indicator is present. Indicators include launch, upward thrust, crouch followed by upward/forward attack, leading with helmet/shoulder/forearm/fist/hand/elbow, or lowering the head before attacking with the crown.

A defenseless player can include a receiver attempting to catch a pass, a player who has relaxed after the play, a passer during or just after a throw, a kick receiver focused on the ball, or a player whose forward progress is stopped.

Video keys: exact contact point, whether contact is forcible, player posture, targeting indicators, defenseless status, and whether the crown of the helmet is used.
