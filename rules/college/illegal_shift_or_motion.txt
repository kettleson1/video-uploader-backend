Ruleset: NCAA college football.
Source basis: 2025 NCAA Football Rules and Interpretations, Rule 7, Section 1, Articles 2 through 4.

Illegal shift or illegal motion occurs when Team A fails to meet pre-snap motion and set requirements. After a shift, all Team A players must become set for the required interval before the snap. At the snap, only one Team A player may be in motion, and that player may not be moving toward the opponent's goal line.

Distinguish a shift, which involves one or more players changing position and then becoming set, from legal motion by one back or eligible player. If multiple players are moving at the snap, or the motion player is moving forward, the action is illegal.

Video keys: player movement before the snap, whether all players pause and become set, number of players moving at the snap, motion direction, and snap timing.
