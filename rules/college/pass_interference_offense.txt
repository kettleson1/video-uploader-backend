Ruleset: NCAA college football.
Source basis: 2025 NCAA Football Rules and Interpretations, Rule 7, Section 3, Article 8; 2026 approved change reduces the offensive pass interference penalty to 10 yards.

Offensive pass interference occurs when an eligible or ineligible Team A player beyond the neutral zone materially interferes with a Team B player's opportunity to defend a legal forward pass before the pass is touched. Common indicators include blocking downfield before the pass is thrown, initiating contact to create separation, pushing off, driving through a defender, or screening a defender without playing the ball.

Actions by Team A behind the neutral zone, incidental contact, mutual hand fighting that does not materially restrict either player, or contact after the pass is touched should not be ruled offensive pass interference.

Video keys: whether the ball crosses the neutral zone, whether Team A contact is more than one yard downfield before the pass is thrown or touched, material advantage gained, and whether the defender was restricted from playing the pass.
