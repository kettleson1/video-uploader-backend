Ruleset: NCAA college football.
Source basis: 2025 NCAA Football Rules and Interpretations, Rule 9, Section 1, Article 6 and related blocking definitions.

Blocking below the waist is restricted in NCAA football and depends on the player's team, position, direction, zone, and timing. A block is suspect when a player initiates force below the waist outside permitted circumstances, especially from the side or back, toward the opponent's legs, or after the ball has left the blocking zone.

Because NCAA low-block rules have detailed positional exceptions, a ruling should cite the exact alignment and play phase when available. If the clip does not show initial positions or direction clearly, mark the decision uncertain.

Video keys: initial position at the snap, offensive or defensive player, contact point below waist, direction of force, ball location, play phase, and whether the block is from front, side, or back.
