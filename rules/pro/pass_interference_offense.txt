Ruleset: NFL pro football.
Source basis: 2025 NFL Official Playing Rules, Rule 8, Section 5.

Offensive pass interference occurs when an offensive player more than one yard beyond the line of scrimmage significantly hinders an opponent's ability to defend a forward pass thrown from behind the line. Prohibited acts include blocking downfield before the pass arrives, initiating contact to create separation, pushing off, driving through a defender, or picking/screening a defender in a way that prevents coverage.

Incidental contact, hand fighting without material restriction, contact within one yard of the line, or contact after the pass is touched should not be treated as pass interference.

Video keys: depth beyond the line, timing relative to pass release and arrival, whether contact creates separation, defender's opportunity to play the ball, and whether the ball has been touched.
