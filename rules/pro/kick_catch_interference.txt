Ruleset: NFL pro football.
Source basis: 2025 NFL Official Playing Rules, Rule 10, opportunity to catch a kick.

Kick-catch interference occurs when the kicking team interferes with the receiver's opportunity to catch a kick before the receiver touches the ball. The receiver must be given a clear opportunity to complete the catch, and contact, obstruction, or forced movement before the catch can be a foul.

Consider whether the opponent was blocked into the receiver, whether the receiver muffed the ball before contact, and whether the kick had already touched the ground or another player.

Video keys: receiver position and focus, ball arrival, contact or obstruction timing, fair-catch signal if any, block into the receiver, and whether the receiver first touches or muffs the kick.
